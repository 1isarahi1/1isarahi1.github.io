📱 SOEN 357 Mini Project: Health Companion Super App

By: Sarah Malik

This UX/UI case study documents my design process for the Health Companion Super App , a mobile health assistant for tracking medications and appointments.

The study covers User Research, Persona Creation, User Journey Mapping, Wireframing, Prototyping, and Usability Testing .

🔍 Phase 1: User Research & Personas

📌 Goal: Understand target users & pain points.

🎯 Target Users

  • Individuals with chronic illnesses (e.g., diabetes, hypertension).
  • Elderly users who need simplified digital solutions.
  • Busy professionals struggling with health management.
  • Caregivers managing family members' medications.

👤 User Personas

John Doe (58, Type 2 Diabetes) – Needs simple reminders and doctor appointment scheduling.

Jemima Joe (45, Busy Professional with Hypertension) – Prefers smartwatch integration and non-intrusive notifications.

Sam Smith (65, COPD Patient) – Requires voice assistance, large-text UI, and an SOS button.

🗺️ Phase 2: User Journey Mapping

📌 Goal: Identify user frustrations & opportunities for UX improvement.

👀 Key Issues Identified

  • Confusing onboarding process for elderly users.
  • Excessive notifications disrupting workflows.
  • Emergency button not easily accessible.

📈 Improvements

  • ✔️ Guided onboarding tutorial added.
  • ✔️ Adjustable notification frequency settings.
  • ✔️ Emergency SOS button relocated to home screen.

🧑‍💻 Phase 4: Usability Testing

📌 Goal: Improve UI based on real user feedback.

🔍 Key Findings

  • 🚫 Complex onboarding – Added a step-by-step guide .
  • 👀 Tiny text – Increased font size and contrast .
  • 🔔 Too many notifications – Users can now adjust frequency .
  • ⚠️ Hidden emergency button – Relocated for quick access .
